# Aokkorn

Aokkorn records personal expenses and coordinates how shared expenses are allocated, requested, and confirmed between people.

## Language

**User**:
A person who signs in with LINE and owns their personal expenses, bills, and receiving channels.
_Avoid_: Account, owner account

**Payment Claim**:
A participant's statement that they have paid an amount, optionally with a transfer slip or note; it is not a confirmed payment until the bill owner accepts it.
_Avoid_: Payment, settlement, paid

**Confirmed Payment**:
A Payment Claim that the bill owner has reviewed and accepted as correct.
_Avoid_: Claim, notification
